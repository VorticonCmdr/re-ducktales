# Original treasure-award presentation

The previous port entered the award reader and requested Sound49 immediately
after an expedition returned. The original inserts visible striped transitions
and a portrait copy first. The restored sequence is shared by successful cave,
jungle and mountain expeditions that reach the ordinary treasure-award branch.
Photo-score bypasses retain their separate original path.

Supported identity is the22-file pairing in `reference/build.json`.
`reference/award-presentation-source.json` records371 original/prepared/Ghidra
bytes and8 loader relocations in explicit INVESTMENT_CODE.EXE, actual x86 LE16
real-mode resident code. No analysis image is run. The existing EGA transition
and primitive proofs are pinned rather than replaced.

Main0b39..0c48 selects picture helper6. Its common preface clears the draw page
and calls1fe0, the EGA striped wipe. Case6 loads MAIN35 and copies320×200 pixels;
helper016d flushes input. Main draws its labels, commits the treasure cash and
draws the value, then calls1fe0 and1fdc before the guarded49 and shared09f5(40).
Each striped call has five groups and five retraces per group:75 retraces total.
There is no input reader or audio-completion wait inside that presentation.

`AwardPresentation` advances independently of the DOM, browser clock and audio
device. It receives driver/retrace events and an explicit work clock. Cash is
committed once after the first portrait copy. Final reveal publication precedes
the same-time continuation that samples sound enabled and enters the reader.
Both native key flushes discard early P/Q/S and Fire; fresh input is needed at
the reader. Restart cancels pending work and cannot emit the abandoned cue.

Rendering uses the expedition's committed final page as the first wipe source,
even when the expedition returns on the same retrace as that publication. The
middle wipe is black; the final reveal exposes the original portrait and text.
Rendering supplies no simulation values. Screen controls and the outside-screen
Fire prompt become available after the reveal.

The established full-page fill/copy budgets are charged. Resource loading, text
drawing, striped-write CPU costs and remaining IRQ/CPU phase are still omitted.
75 display retraces are statically established; uninterrupted native elapsed
time and new original intermediate screenshots are not established here.

This intentional timing correction invalidates the old fixed-timestamp award
and full-campaign recordings on the changed runtime. They remain historical,
unmodified evidence. They must not be accepted by skipping the new presentation
or relaxing state/timer comparisons. Fresh focused browser verification is
pending; the implementation and available commands alone are not acceptance.
