Space / Enter: fire · Arrow keys: select · In the office, press Up + Space to enter the money bin; Right + Space opens the map. Click the map to place the cursor, then fire to inspect a destination. Left / Right select Map or Travel; fire confirms. Escape returns to the map or office before departure is confirmed. In flight, hold Space to accelerate; Down raises the nose and Up lowers it. Release Space to slow down. In caves, hold an arrow to point and press Space to move through a passage. In mountains, arrows move Scrooge. Hold Fire to swing the climbing hook, then release to throw. Up climbs ledges or uses a transport point. In jungles, arrows walk, climb or drop; Fire jumps.
